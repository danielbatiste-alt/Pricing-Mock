Questions people actually ask

What counts as a response?

One completed survey of up to 50 questions. We meter the answers, not the interviews, so a 100-question survey uses two responses of your allowance.

You publish the software but not the fieldwork. Why?

Because a single number would be wrong for almost everyone. A short survey to a broad audience and a long one to a narrow audience are different jobs at different costs. We would rather quote it accurately in a day than publish a figure you cannot rely on.

Can I start without talking to anyone?

Starter is card-payable and live the same day. Team and Professional take an invoice on 30-day terms.

What if we only need one study?

Then let our team run it, and MiHQ comes with it. What you spend on the project comes off your first licence if you subscribe within 90 days.

Which markets do you cover?

Our own panel covers Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, the Philippines and Vietnam. Partner reach extends to more than 150 countries.

Do prices change when we grow?

Seats get cheaper as you move up, and so does the fieldwork rate. Growing with us always costs less than starting again.

Tell us the study, not your budget

Describe what you are trying to find out and we will come back with the plan, the fieldwork cost and whether our team should run it. One working day, in writing, whether or not you buy.
