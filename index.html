Questions people ask

What counts as a response?+
One completed survey of up to 50 questions. We count answers rather than interviews, so a 100-question survey uses two responses of your allowance.
How do we pay?+
Starter is paid by card, upfront for the year. Team and Professional are invoiced annually in advance on 30-day terms. Enterprise and Global are invoiced quarterly in advance.
Do our own contacts count?+
Yes. Responses from your own list use your allowance. If we send the invitations for you, that is charged per 1,000 emails, as listed above.
Which markets do you cover?+
Our own panel covers Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, the Philippines and Vietnam. Partner panels extend reach to more than 150 countries.
What happens as we grow?+
Extra seats cost less on higher plans, and the discount on panel rates rises from 5% on Team to 20% on Global.
How does fieldwork credit work?+
Team and above include credit to spend on respondents from our panel. It is drawn at the quoted rate for each study.
